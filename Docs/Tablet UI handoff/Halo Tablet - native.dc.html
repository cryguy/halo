HALO NATIVE · TABLET

The app at tablet width: one rail, wider gutters, denser art

Nothing new in the visual system — same colours, radii, type and components as HaloTheme.kt. What changes is the frame: the floating bottom bar becomes a left rail in landscape, browse screens put the search field and filter beside the title instead of stacked under it, posters run seven across, the detail screen keeps its full-bleed hero and lays episodes out in a grid, and the sources picker arrives as a right rail over the title rather than as its own screen.
